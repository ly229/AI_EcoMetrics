# David Autor

Official page: [MIT Economics profile](https://economics.mit.edu/people/faculty/david-h-autor)

## Bio
David Autor is a labor economist known for influential work on computerization, trade exposure, job polarization, and the distributional consequences of technological change.

## Focus
- Labor-market impacts of technology
- Skill polarization and task change
- Trade and employment adjustment

## Selected Works
- "The Skill Content of Recent Technological Change" (2003)
- "The Polarization of the U.S. Labor Market" (2006)
- "Why Are There Still So Many Jobs?" (2015)
- "The China Shock" (2016)
