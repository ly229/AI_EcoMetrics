# David Autor

Keywords: labor markets, skill polarization, globalization

Official page: [MIT Economics profile](https://economics.mit.edu/people/faculty/david-h-autor)

## Bio
MIT labor economist recognized for influential work on job polarization, the labor-market effects of trade, and how technology changes the demand for skills.

## Focus
- Labor markets
- Skill polarization
- Trade and globalization

## Selected Works

- "The Polarization of the U.S. Labor Market" (2006)
- "The China Syndrome: Local Labor Market Effects of Import Competition in the United States" with David Dorn and Gordon Hanson (2013)
