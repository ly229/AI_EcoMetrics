# Why Are There Still So Many Jobs?

Author: David H. Autor
Year: 2015
Source: [MIT Economics profile](https://economics.mit.edu/people/faculty/david-h-autor)

## Key Idea
The paper argues that automation replaces tasks, not entire jobs. Because many jobs combine routine and nonroutine tasks, labor can remain valuable even as particular tasks are automated.

## Data
This is a conceptual review grounded in historical and contemporary labor-market evidence on automation, task change, and occupational evolution.

## Model / Method
The paper uses a task-based framework to explain why automation can both substitute for labor and create new demand for human tasks.

## Main Takeaway
Automation changes the composition of work more than the existence of work itself. As machines take over routine tasks, labor shifts toward judgment, interaction, and problem-solving tasks that remain hard to codify.

## Why It Matters For This Study
It is one of the most useful papers for interpreting how agent-AI may augment some workers while displacing specific tasks.
