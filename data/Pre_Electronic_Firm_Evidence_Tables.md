## Summary Interpretation
- **Merrill Lynch**: Large retail brokerage with 40k+ staff by early 1980s, broad division structure [web:105][web:111].
- **Morgan Stanley**: Partnership growth from 13 to 1,700 staff (1970-1980), investment banking focus [web:94][web:133].
- **Smith Barney**: Merger-driven firm with 700+ staff in 1930s, sales-heavy by 1970s [web:139][web:135].
