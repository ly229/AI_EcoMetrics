# Economics of the Datacenter: Capex, Power, and Neoclouds

Source tweet: [apoorv03](https://x.com/apoorv03/status/2047082162395177428?s=20)

Related analysis: [The Economics of Generative AI: Two Years Later](https://apoorv03.com/p/the-economics-of-generative-ai-two)

## Summary

This tweet points to a capital-allocation view of the AI boom: where the roughly US$650B of AI infrastructure capex is going, who is capturing the margin, and which constraint matters next.

The core argument is that the bottleneck has shifted from GPUs to power, and that the next phase of value capture may depend less on raw chip supply and more on datacenter siting, energy access, interconnects, and the economics of neo-cloud providers.

## Main Questions

- Where is the AI infrastructure capex actually flowing this year?
- Which firms are capturing margins versus absorbing cost pressure?
- How does the binding constraint move from GPUs to power and grid capacity?
- What business models work in the neocloud layer?

## Why It Matters

- It reframes AI as an infrastructure and financing cycle, not just a model cycle
- It highlights that scarcity can move downstream from chips to electricity and datacenter capacity
- It helps explain why capital allocation, not just technical progress, will shape the next phase of the market
- It provides a second perspective to the autonomous lab note: industrial automation at the edge of chemistry versus infrastructure scaling at the edge of compute

## Economics Framing

- Semiconductor vendors may capture early-cycle hardware rents
- Hyperscalers and neoclouds compete on access to power, capital, and deployment speed
- Rising power demand can compress margins if energy, cooling, and grid connection costs grow faster than utilization
- The biggest winners may be the firms that control scarce infrastructure, financing, and long-duration contracts

## Relevance

This is useful for thinking about where AI spending turns into durable economic value, and where it gets trapped in fixed-cost infrastructure buildout.
