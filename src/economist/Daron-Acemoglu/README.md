# Daron Acemoglu

Keywords: institutions, growth, automation

Official page: [MIT Economics profile](https://economics.mit.edu/people/faculty/daron-acemoglu)

## Bio
MIT economist whose work spans political economy, institutions, economic growth, and the labor-market consequences of automation and technology.

## Focus
- Institutions and development
- Growth theory
- Automation, technology, and labor markets

## Selected Works
- "The Colonial Origins of Comparative Development" with Simon Johnson and James A. Robinson (2001)
- *Why Nations Fail* with James A. Robinson (2012)
- *The Narrow Corridor* with James A. Robinson (2019)

## Most Relevant to This Study
- "Tasks at Work: Comparative Advantage, Technology and Labor Demand" with Fredric Kong and Pascual Restrepo (2024)
- "Building Pro-Worker Artificial Intelligence" with David Autor and Simon Johnson (2026)
- "Automation and Polarization" with Jonas Loebbing (2026)
- "AI, Human Cognition and Knowledge Collapse" with Dingwen Kong and Asuman Ozdaglar (2026)
