# Andrew McAfee Citations

## Selected Citations

- Brynjolfsson, E., & McAfee, A. (2017). *The Business of Artificial Intelligence: What it Can and Cannot Do for Your Organization*. *Harvard Business Review*.
- McAfee, A., & Brynjolfsson, E. (2016). *Human Work in the Robotic Future: Policy for the Age of Automation*. *Foreign Affairs*, July/August 2016.
- Brynjolfsson, E., & McAfee, A. (2015). *Will Humans Go the Way of Horses? Labor in the Second Machine Age*. *Foreign Affairs*, July/August 2015.
- Brynjolfsson, E., McAfee, A., & Spence, M. (2014). *New World Order: Labor, Capital, and Ideas in the Power Law Economy*. *Foreign Affairs*, July/August 2014.

## Notes

- Use the HBR citation for business AI and organizational adoption.
- Use the Foreign Affairs citations for labor displacement, policy, and the distributional effects of automation.
- Use the New World Order citation for the power-law economy and the role of ideas.
