# Race Against the Machine

Author: Andrew McAfee and Erik Brynjolfsson
Year: 2011
Source: [MIT Press Bookstore](https://mitpressbookstore.mit.edu/book/9780984725113)

## Key Idea
The book argues that digital technologies were accelerating innovation and productivity while also automating many human tasks. It warns that the labor market may not automatically share the gains from technology.

## Data
The argument is built from evidence on digitization, productivity, employment trends, and the changing economics of computerization.

## Model / Method
The book uses a task-substitution and skill-biased technical change logic. It is a synthesis rather than a formal empirical paper.

## Main Takeaway
Automation can be highly productive while still causing painful labor displacement. The policy challenge is to make worker adjustment faster than machine substitution.

## Why It Matters For This Study
It is one of the clearest statements that automation can be economically powerful while still displacing workers.
