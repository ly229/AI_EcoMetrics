# The Geek Way

Author: Andrew McAfee
Year: 2023
Source: [MIT IDE publication page](https://ide.mit.edu/publication/the-geek-way/)

## Key Idea
The book argues that high-performing organizations are increasingly built around norms of speed, ownership, science, and openness. This matters for labor because organizational design shapes how quickly AI and automation are adopted and how workers are coordinated with new tools.

## Data
The book draws on corporate examples, management practice, and technology adoption patterns.

## Model / Method
The framework is organizational and managerial rather than econometric. It links culture and governance to innovation performance.

## Main Takeaway
The speed of AI adoption depends on management norms and experimentation. Firms that can iterate quickly and redesign workflows are more likely to turn new technology into productivity gains instead of simple headcount reduction.

## Why It Matters For This Study
It helps explain why some firms may adopt agentic AI in worker-augmenting ways while others use it primarily for replacement.
