# More from Less

Author: Andrew McAfee
Year: 2019
Source: [MIT IDE publication page](https://ide.mit.edu/publication/more-from-less/)

## Key Idea
The book argues that modern technological progress allows societies to produce more prosperity with fewer natural resources. The broader relevance here is that technology can increase efficiency without necessarily requiring more labor or more material input.

## Data
The book is a historical and empirical synthesis of resource use, growth, and environmental efficiency.

## Model / Method
The book is argumentative and evidence-driven, tying together patterns in technological change, market systems, and productivity.

## Main Takeaway
Technological progress can decouple economic growth from resource intensity. More broadly, it shows that innovation can create efficiency gains that are not captured by older measures of production.

## Why It Matters For This Study
It is relevant because it shows how technological progress can decouple output from traditional constraints, reinforcing the idea that AI can transform production functions.
