# Erik Brynjolfsson

Official page: [Personal site](https://www.brynjolfsson.com/about)

## Bio
Erik Brynjolfsson is a leading economist of technology and AI whose work examines how information technologies, digital platforms, and machine learning reshape productivity, firms, and labor markets.

## Focus
- Technology adoption and productivity
- AI and digital transformation
- Labor-market effects of automation

## Selected Works
- "The Productivity Paradox of Information Technology" (1993)
- *Race Against the Machine* with Andrew McAfee (2011)
- *The Second Machine Age* with Andrew McAfee (2014)
- "Artificial Intelligence and the Modern Productivity Paradox" with Daniel Rock and Chad Syverson (2017)
