# Erik Brynjolfsson

Keywords: digital economy, productivity, AI

Official page: [Personal site](https://www.brynjolfsson.com/about)

## Bio
Economist and MIT scholar focused on the digital economy, measuring productivity, and the economic effects of information technology and AI.

## Focus
- Digital transformation
- Productivity measurement
- Artificial intelligence and labor markets

## Selected Works
