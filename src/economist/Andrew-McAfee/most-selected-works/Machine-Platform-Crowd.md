# Machine, Platform, Crowd

Author: Andrew McAfee and Erik Brynjolfsson
Year: 2017
Source: [MIT IDE publication page](https://ide.mit.edu/publication/the-geek-way/)

## Key Idea
The book explains how machine capabilities, digital platforms, and crowd-based coordination are reshaping firms and markets. It argues that these trends change how value is created and how work is organized.

## Data
This is a broad synthesis based on examples from digital firms, platforms, and knowledge-intensive work.

## Model / Method
The framework combines automation, platform economics, and crowdsourcing. It is conceptual and strategic rather than econometric.

## Main Takeaway
Digital technologies reorganize production by changing who does the work, where it is done, and how it is coordinated. That makes labor-market effects depend on firm structure as much as on the underlying machine capability.

## Why It Matters For This Study
It helps explain how digital systems can reorganize labor demand by shifting work toward platforms, ecosystems, and machine-assisted workflows.
