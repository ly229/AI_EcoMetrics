# Building Pro-Worker Artificial Intelligence

Author: Daron Acemoglu, David Autor, and Simon Johnson
Year: 2026
Source: [MIT Economics PDF](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2026-03/Building%20Pro-Worker%20Artificial%20Intelligence.pdf)

## Key Idea
The paper argues that AI can be designed to expand worker capabilities instead of simply replacing labor. It proposes a "pro-worker" direction for AI that raises the value of human expertise and widens labor-market opportunities.

## Data
This is a conceptual and policy-oriented paper drawing on recent evidence on AI adoption, labor-market inequality, task automation, and productivity effects.

## Model / Method
The paper is framed as an argument about incentives, technology design, and policy. It contrasts worker-augmenting AI with labor-displacing AI and discusses underinvestment in worker-friendly technology.

## Main Takeaway
The paper argues that private incentives may favor labor-saving AI over worker-augmenting AI, so the direction of AI development is partly a governance problem. If the goal is broad-based gains, policy and design choices matter.

## Why It Matters For This Study
It is directly aligned with the question of whether agent-AI will augment or replace workers.
