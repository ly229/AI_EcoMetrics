# Generative AI at Work

Author: Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li, and Lindsey Raymond
Year: 2023
Source: [NBER Working Paper 31161](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161)

## Key Idea
The paper studies how generative AI affects workplace performance. It shows that AI can raise productivity, especially for less-experienced workers, by providing real-time support, advice, and task augmentation.

## Data
The paper uses workplace data from a real-world setting where workers interacted with a generative AI tool. The analysis compares outcomes before and after access to the tool and across worker experience groups.

## Model / Method
The design is an empirical field setting with quasi-experimental variation in AI access. The key comparison is between treated and untreated workers, with attention to heterogeneous effects by skill and experience.

## Main Takeaway
The main labor-market implication is augmentation, not only substitution. Generative AI can raise the floor for novice workers, reduce search and drafting costs, and make expert-like support available at scale.

## Why It Matters For This Study
It is highly relevant because it shows a concrete channel through which agentic AI can augment labor rather than simply replace it.
