# Andrew McAfee

Official page: [MIT IDE profile](https://ide.mit.edu/people/andrew-mcafee/)

## Bio
Andrew McAfee studies how digital technologies, machine intelligence, and organizational change affect productivity, competition, and the future of work.

## Focus
- Technology and productivity
- Automation and machine substitution
- Business organization and labor change
