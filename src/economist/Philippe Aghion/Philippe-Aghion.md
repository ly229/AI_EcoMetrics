# Philippe Aghion

Keywords: innovation, growth, industrial policy

Official page: [Harvard CES profile](https://ces.fas.harvard.edu/people/000066-philippe-aghion)

## Bio
French economist known for work on endogenous growth, innovation, competition, and how industrial policy shapes long-run productivity.

## Focus
- Endogenous growth
- Innovation and firm dynamics
- Competition policy and industrial policy

## Selected Works

- *The Economics of Growth* (2008)
- "What Do We Learn From Schumpeterian Growth Theory?" (2013)
